Vanilla Bow Tiers 0.2.6.1

Projectile render hotfix:
- Corrected the sign of the flying arrow's pitch calculation.
- Minecraft's X rotation convention is positive downward, while positive Y
  velocity means upward motion. The previous correction used the opposite
  convention and could make the rendered projectile visually diverge downward
  from the actual arrow entity/collision path.
- Projectile physics, damage, Sharpshooter balance, textures, and Bray nocked
  arrow compatibility are unchanged.
